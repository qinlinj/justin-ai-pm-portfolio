Services and method examples↓

Agent product design

A written job spec: who sits in the seat, what arrives, what “done” looks like, and the gate the agent cannot cross.

Agent build

The first seat ships. The agent may draft, retrieve, route, classify, and queue; nothing irreversible without a named person.

Agent operations

Evals against the job, not a generic model scoreboard. Tighten the gate where the workflow still fails; improve the record so a proposal can be reopened.
