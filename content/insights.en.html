The AI product questions
I want to own.

My view: useful AI products need a reliable path from answers to actions. Technical AI PM work means defining the job, the evidence of success, and the limits of autonomy—not just selecting a model.

01 / SCOPE

What should the system own?

Separate answering from acting. Define the job, the tools, and the point where a person must decide.

02 / EVIDENCE

What counts as a good outcome?

Evaluate the task, attribute failures to a system stage, and compare quality with latency and cost.

03 / CONTROL

Can a decision be reviewed?

Keep sources, approvals, and action records visible. Make a proposal easy to inspect before it becomes an irreversible action.
