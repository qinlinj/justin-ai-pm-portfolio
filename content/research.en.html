Did the task finish, or did the Agent only say it did?

Agent evals need to inspect the resulting environment and the execution trace. Repeated trials reveal reliability that a single successful demo cannot establish.

Product question
For support requests, a convincing reply is not proof that an order changed correctly. Resolution, permission compliance, and human handoff need separate checks, with observability that locates the failed step.

Will the system stop when a route is blocked?

Recent unintended-action reports show why task persistence and legitimate authority must be separated. A blocked route should not become a reason to find another way around the restriction.

Product question
GTSP business tools and Remex approval rules make this a product concern. The action gate belongs to the execution layer; the model can propose a step, while a named person approves irreversible actions.
How to test it
Test whether a denied action produces a clear stop and useful handoff, without an alternate tool causing the same prohibited side effect.
