## Hiring assessment

For a technical AI PM or agent product role, the portfolio provides enough substance to justify an initial technical conversation: real full-stack delivery, explicit ownership of integration work, human approval boundaries, and acceptance activities. It is less persuasive for a senior product role requiring evidence of market selection, prioritization, commercial outcomes, and organizational leadership. This is an assessment of what the portfolio shows, not of the owner's unlisted abilities.

The main remaining gap is inspectable evidence. A reviewer can read what happened, but cannot yet inspect a sanitized input/output pair, a real interface, a decision artifact, or a documented iteration. More architecture terminology or decorative interaction would not close that gap.
